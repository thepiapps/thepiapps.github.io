Home Launcher - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026  ·  Application: Home Launcher (Glass Edition)

1. Privacy Commitment

Home Launcher is committed to safeguarding your privacy. We believe that your home screen and your personal phone usage belong exclusively to you. Our architecture is offline-first: we do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal data or usage habits to any third-party data brokers.

2. Data Processed On-Device

To deliver a seamless launcher experience, Home Launcher accesses and processes certain device information locally on your device without transmitting it to our servers:

  • Installed Applications: We query installed applications to display your home grid, app drawer, search results, and category filters. This data stays entirely on your phone.
  • Customizations & Themes: Your icon shapes, grid sizing, folder groupings, and selected themes are stored locally in your private sandboxed app database.
  • System Widgets: Home screen widgets communicate directly with Android's AppWidget subsystem and are not tracked or monitored by Home Launcher.

3. Permissions & Declarations

Home Launcher requests specific system permissions to function as an Android launcher. We clearly declare why each permission is utilized:

  • Default Launcher (Role): Required so pressing the Home button or using navigation gestures returns you directly to Home Launcher.
  • Query All Packages: Essential on Android 11+ to detect installed applications and allow launching them from the drawer and home screen.
  • Set Wallpaper: Allows applying curated glass and dynamic theme wallpapers to your desktop and lock screen.
  • Expand Status Bar: Allows convenience gestures (such as swiping down on the desktop) to expand the Android notification shade.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The free edition of Home Launcher integrates standard Google Play services to support continuous development:

  • Google AdMob: May display contextual or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and your AdMob consent preferences.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Used to deliver remote configuration flags, theme updates, and performance optimizations securely.

5. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not operate external user account databases, we do not store your personal information on remote servers. All your launcher preferences remain in Android's sandboxed local storage and are automatically cleared if you choose to uninstall the application.

6. Children's Privacy

Home Launcher is safe for general audiences and complies fully with COPPA and Google Play Families policy requirements. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

7. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or Home Launcher's privacy practices, please contact us at officialpiapps@gmail.com.
