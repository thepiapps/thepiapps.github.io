The Pi Apps ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application DJ Music Mixer (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. By using DJ Music Mixer, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Permissions & Data Collection

To deliver real-time DJ mixing, audio playback, sound effects, and recording functionality, the App requires certain device permissions:

a. Audio Files & Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to enable you to browse, select, import, and mix audio tracks stored on your device.
  • Data Handling: All audio processing and mixing occurs locally on your device. We DO NOT upload, store, collect, or transmit your personal music files to any external servers or third parties.

b. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: Used to deliver optional push notifications regarding app updates, new DJ sound packs, mixing tips, and important service announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Users can grant or revoke this at any time.

c. Internet & Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

  • Purpose: Required for third-party ad serving, app stability monitoring, and crash reporting.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect non-personally identifiable information (such as unique device identifiers, IP addresses, device model, operating system version, and anonymous usage statistics):

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform functionalities. Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display personalized and non-personalized advertisements. Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Used to monitor application stability, track fatal and non-fatal crash reports, and analyze anonymous app performance metrics to improve user experience. Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not collect or store personal identity data on our own servers. Data collected by third-party providers (such as Google and Firebase) is handled under their respective privacy policies and secured with industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) during transit. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information.
