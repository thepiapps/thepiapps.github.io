1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how The Pi Apps ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects information when you download, install, access, or play our mobile application "Arrow Escape: Tap Puzzle Game" (Package Name: com.pi.arrowescape), available on the Google Play Store, and any associated services (collectively referred to as the "App" or "Game").

By installing or using Arrow Escape, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy, please uninstall and discontinue using the App immediately.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Arrow Escape is an offline-friendly puzzle game designed so that you can enjoy the game without creating an account or providing direct personal contact details. However, to provide core gameplay functions, analytics, cloud configurations, crash diagnostic services, and non-intrusive advertising, certain technical data is collected automatically through integrated third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs).

A. Automatically Collected Technical & Device Information

When you access or interact with the App, our integrated third-party service providers automatically collect certain device and network data, including:

  • Device hardware specifications (device model, manufacturer, processor type).
  • Operating system version (Android OS version and API level).
  • Unique device identifiers and network parameters (IP address, network carrier, connection type: Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Screen resolution, system language, locale, and time zone settings.

B. Gameplay & Analytics Information

We collect non-personally identifiable telemetry to understand how players interact with our puzzles and improve game balance:

  • Levels played, completed, retried, or failed.
  • Number of moves made, elapsed level completion duration, and stars earned.
  • In-game power-up usage (e.g., Undo, Hammer, Hints).
  • Virtual currency transactions (earning and spending virtual Star Coins).
  • Game launch timestamps, session length, and frequency of play.

C. Crash Diagnostics & Stability Data

To ensure a stable and bug-free user experience, we collect diagnostic logs when unexpected errors or crashes occur:

  • Stack traces and crash breadcrumbs.
  • Operating state of the device at the time of a crash (free RAM, disk space).
  • App build version and release channel.

D. Cloud Messaging & Notification Tokens

If you grant permission for push notifications on Android 13+ (API level 33+) devices, a unique, randomized Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token is generated to deliver game notifications, event announcements, and daily puzzle reminders. This token does not identify you personally.

E. Information You Provide Directly to Support

If you contact our support team via email, we collect your email address, your name (if provided), and any details included in your correspondence to assist you and resolve your inquiry.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Arrow Escape does NOT collect, store, or process:

  • Real names, home addresses, or telephone numbers (unless you contact support).
  • Precise real-time GPS location.
  • Contacts, address books, or calendar information.
  • Camera, microphone, audio recordings, or photo gallery access.
  • Biometric, health, or sensitive personal data.
  • Credit card, debit card, or banking credentials.

4. How We Use Collected Information

The collected information is used solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and optimize Arrow Escape.
  • To save and synchronize your puzzle progress, achievements, stars, and tools.
  • To detect, diagnose, and resolve technical glitches, performance bottlenecks, and crashes.
  • To dynamically configure game parameters and balance level difficulty via remote configuration.
  • To serve relevant advertisements (banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app open ads) that keep the game free to play.
  • To verify rewarded ad views and grant in-game rewards (e.g., bonus coins, hints).
  • To send optional push notifications regarding daily challenges or updates.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, cheating, and unauthorized exploitation of the App.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and store policies.

6. Android Permissions Explained

Arrow Escape requests only minimal permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the App to connect to the internet to load ads, fetch remote configurations, send crash reports, and update analytics.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App to detect whether a network connection (Wi-Fi or cellular) is active before attempting network calls.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Allows the App to provide subtle haptic feedback when you interact with arrows and solve puzzles (can be toggled off in the in-game Settings menu).
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: On Android 13 (API 33) and above, requests user permission to display notifications for daily challenges and game news. You may decline or disable notifications at any time in system settings.

7. Advertising Choices and Tracking Controls

Arrow Escape displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings:

  • Personalized Ads: Ads tailored to your interests based on your Google Advertising ID and activity across other apps and websites.
  • Non-Personalized Ads: Contextual ads based on current app context rather than past user profiles.

Managing Your Ad Preferences:

  • On Android devices, you can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your Advertising ID at any time by navigating to: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • You can manage your personalized advertising settings across Google services at: https://adssettings.google.com
